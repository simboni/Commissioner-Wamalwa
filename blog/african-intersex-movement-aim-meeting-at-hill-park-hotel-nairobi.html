African Intersex Movement (AIM) Board Meeting at Hill Park Hotel, Nairobi

WCommissioner Dr. Dennis WamalwaKenya National Commission on Human Rights

The African Intersex Movement (AIM) convened a strategic meeting at Hill Park Hotel, Nairobi, bringing together board members to deliberate on the progress, challenges, and future of intersex advocacy on the continent. The meeting, structured to foster engagement and reflection, was a significant step in advancing the rights and recognition of intersex persons in Africa.

Opening and Welcome Address

The event commenced with an arrival and registration session at 9:00 AM, coordinated by Sammie. This was followed by a warm welcome address by the Board Chair, setting the tone for a collaborative and insightful engagement. The chair underscored AIM’s commitment to strengthening intersex advocacy through strategic planning and enhanced stakeholder engagement.

Team Building and Review of Previous Discussions

To break the ice and promote team synergy, Sammie led brief interactive activities, fostering a sense of unity and shared purpose among participants. The Board Secretary then facilitated the review of the last board meeting minutes, ensuring continuity and accountability in AIM’s governance processes.

Setting the Agenda and Work Plan Review

Following the review, the Board Secretary guided members through the adoption of the meeting agenda and outlined the objectives of the discussions. A detailed report on AIM’s 2024 work plan was then presented by Sammie, offering insights into the organization’s strategic initiatives and planned activities for the year.

Financial Report and Organizational Retrospect

The Board Treasurer provided a comprehensive financial report for 2024, highlighting key expenditures, funding sources, and budgetary priorities. This was followed by a reflective session led by the Board Chair, who evaluated AIM’s progress over the past year, acknowledging milestones while identifying areas for improvement.

Lunch and Governance Discussions

A lunch break provided an opportunity for informal networking and discussions among attendees. The afternoon session resumed with a critical discussion on the roles and responsibilities of the Board of Directors, led by the Board Chair. This session aimed at reinforcing good governance and strengthening the leadership structure of AIM to ensure the sustainability of its advocacy work.

Conclusion

The AIM meeting at Hill Park Hotel was a crucial platform for assessing the organization's trajectory and reinforcing its commitment to advancing intersex rights in Africa. The deliberations underscored the importance of collaboration, strategic planning, and financial prudence in sustaining advocacy efforts. With a clear roadmap set for 2025, AIM remains steadfast in its mission to champion the rights, dignity, and inclusion of intersex persons across the continent.
