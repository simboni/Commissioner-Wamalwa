THE PUBLIC INQUIRY INTO THE IMPACT OF BUSINESS OPERATIONS ON THE RIGHTS OF INDIGENOUS PEOPLE IN THE NORTH RIFT REGION.

WCommissioner Dr. Dennis WamalwaKenya National Commission on Human Rights

Introduction

From 23rd July to 31st July 2024, Commissioner Dr. Wamalwa Dennis, participated in a Public Inquiry organized by the Kenya National Commission on Human Rights (KNCHR) to examine the impact of business operations on the rights of Indigenous People in the North Rift Region. This inquiry covered Turkana, West Pokot, Baringo, Elgeyo- Marakwet, and Nandi counties. The inquiry aimed to gather firsthand information, assess the socio-economic effects of business activities, and formulate recommendations for promoting and protecting the rights of Indigenous communities affected by these operations.

Objectives of the Public Inquiry

  • To investigate the extent to which business operations have affected the cultural, economic, and social rights of Indigenous communities in the North Rift Region.
  • To provide a platform for Indigenous People to voice their concerns regarding the impacts of these operations on their livelihoods.
  • To assess the compliance of business operations with national and international human rights standards.
  • To make recommendations aimed at mitigating negative impacts and ensuring the protection of Indigenous rights.

Activities Undertaken

Stakeholder Engagements:

  • Engaged with various stakeholders, including community leaders, local government officials, and representatives from businesses operating in the region.
  • Held community forums in Turkana, West Pokot, Baringo, Elgeyo-Marakwet, and Nandi counties to collect testimonies from affected Indigenous people.

Documentation of Human Rights Violations:

  • Documented cases of human rights violations related to land dispossession, environmental degradation, and displacement of Indigenous communities.
  • Highlighted the specific challenges faced by women, children, and other vulnerable groups within these communities.

Recommendations Development:

  • Formulated recommendations addressing the gaps identified during the inquiry.
  • Proposed measures for ensuring that businesses operating in the region adhere to human rights standards and contribute positively to the well-being of Indigenous communities.

Findings and Observations

The inquiry revealed several key issues affecting Indigenous communities in the North Rift Region:

  • Land Dispossession: Many Indigenous communities have experienced loss of ancestral lands due to business operations, leading to displacement and loss of livelihoods.
  • Environmental Degradation: Business activities, particularly in mining and agriculture, have caused significant environmental damage, affecting water sources and agricultural productivity.
  • Social and Economic Marginalization: Indigenous communities have been largely excluded from the economic benefits of business operations in their regions. There is a lack of inclusive development that considers the needs and rights of these communities.
  • Human Rights Violations: Cases of intimidation, lack of consultation, and inadequate compensation for displaced communities were documented.

Conclusion

The Public Inquiry into the Impact of Business Operations on the Rights of Indigenous People in the North Rift Region has provided valuable insights into the challenges faced by these communities. The findings underscore the need for stronger safeguards to protect Indigenous rights and ensure that business operations contribute to sustainable development. Moving forward, it is crucial for all stakeholders to work together in implementing the recommendations of this inquiry to promote justice, equity, and respect for human rights in the region.
